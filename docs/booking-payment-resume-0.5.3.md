The customer returned through email to a cart-ready small-knife pickup request and received “Use the original booking payment browser.” That request is separate from the cancelled unpaid integration test. Booking references, native order IDs, appointment confirmation and payment states must remain separate.

## Implementation instructions and checks

1. Keep the existing booking and listing intent. Do not create a replacement booking, order, gateway attempt or payment session to repair a missing cart. Never reset `creation_started`, order links or processor evidence.
2. GET displays the checkout or a private recovery form. It never prepares a cart or renews capacity. Require the existing booking grant, first-party POST, intent-specific CSRF and explicit saved-details consent to resume. A plain booking reference is insufficient authentication; explain that the customer should open the private email link. Keep personal data out of URLs and screenshots published to GitHub.
3. Acquire the booking lock and original listing lock. Check reciprocal booking/intent ownership and current booking state. Reject orders linked in durable data, interrupted creation, observed payment, and native orders found by either intent or booking metadata, including cancelled, draft and trashed orders. Existing financial attempts require original-order review rather than this recovery path.
4. Preserve unrelated carts and pending orders. A matching, bound, unstarted cart may be refreshed; an empty browser may receive the original intent. Rebind that intent to the current browser so the older browser cannot submit a second checkout. Keep the original quote validation and order-creation locks at final payment.
5. Recheck current consent, county/ZIP coverage, services, fees, policy, day and capacity. Renew only one existing unpaid booking hold if its window expired and capacity remains. Refresh pricing in native WooCommerce, then prepare the original intent’s cart. Show the full native total before payment. No merchant confirmation, email, invoice or financial request occurs during recovery.
6. A general shopper token may expire before the independent booking grant. Do not extend or revive the general token. Scoped native booking validation requires the original reciprocal booking/intent, current browser binding and booking selection. Generic marketplace access and its expiry rules stay unchanged.
7. Replace the browser-only dead end with “Pick up where you left off” and **Continue to secure payment**. Explain that the booking is saved, that no order is linked yet, and that the customer reviews the total before approving payment. A closed booking or linked/uncertain order must not offer replacement checkout.
8. Test classic and HPOS storage with synchronization on/off using the fenced synthetic database. Exercise replay, wrong owner, expiration, unavailable capacity, unrelated cart, pending order, new/old browser bindings, interrupted creation, orphan native orders, revoked consent, paid/cancelled bookings and expired general-token/native booking isolation. Verify the browser POST-to-checkout path without clicking Place order.
9. Package the existing plugin as 0.5.3, retain the exact 0.5.2 rollback ZIP, deploy through native WordPress replacement and read back critical source. Preserve credentials, payment mode, webhooks, capacity and pickup coverage. Do not create a production customer, send another notification or pay/refund as deployment verification.
