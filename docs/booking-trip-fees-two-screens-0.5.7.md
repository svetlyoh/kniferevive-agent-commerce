# Per-order trip fees and two-screen booking — 0.5.7

Owner approval: single pickup or delivery is $6 per order; the previously approved $11 combined pickup/return delivery price stays. Sharpening always costs extra. Customer drop-off and collection have a $0 trip fee, including when paying at collection. The unpaid choice is last.

The five discovery/form choices are prepaid customer drop-off/collection, prepaid customer drop-off with “we bring it back” delivery, merchant pickup/customer collection, pickup/return delivery “comeback combo”, then customer drop-off/collection with payment at pickup. `prepaid_dropoff_delivery` is a distinct API mode that implies return delivery; canonical storage keeps drop-off plus courier delivery. Eligible ZIPs and verified payment are required for all four prepaid choices before seller submission. Unpaid customer drop-off skips ZIP checking.

Trip pricing uses one native taxable fee per order, not the $7.99 parcel flat-rate policy or a multiplier per knife. The service-only handoff remains zero-cost for native seller Local Pickup classification. New records freeze trip taxability and tax class alongside their existing frozen fee. Existing pre-0.5.7 records retain their prior non-taxable fee treatment and original amounts; no old order or quoted amount is migrated. Existing store tax rates remain authoritative. Production tax calculation was already enabled; only `booking_transport_taxable` was changed from false to true. Pickup ZIPs, fees, capacity, gateway mode and general shipping policies were not changed.

The details screen is “Your knife game plan”. Its editable services, handoff, requested day, contact/trip address and optional billing disclosure lead directly to KnifeRevive secure payment. The authorized submission prepares an independent native checkout session without replacing the ordinary store cart. The repeated technical booking/payment/order state box is removed. A readable compact price summary and semantic expandable review replace the duplicated section 4 prose. The payment screen retains final tax, full total, privacy/cancellation terms and the installed gateway's payment controls. “Ship to a different address?” is removed only on authenticated booking checkout; native AJAX and order-data hooks preserve the consent-bound service address when billing differs. Existing receipt, cancellation/refund and interrupted-checkout recovery paths remain.
